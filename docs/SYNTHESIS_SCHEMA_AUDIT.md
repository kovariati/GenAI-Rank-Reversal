# Seven-synthesis reporting-schema audit

The peer-reviewed article reports only the bounded headline result. The complete audit is retained here in machine-readable form.

## Frozen inclusion rule

A synthesis had to: (1) be a quantitative meta-analysis or systematic quantitative synthesis of experimental or quasi-experimental Generative AI/ChatGPT interventions in education; (2) report learner performance, achievement, knowledge, learning, or a closely related educational outcome; (3) be peer reviewed/finalized and publicly available by 17 August 2026; (4) provide sufficiently accessible published coding, moderator, extraction, or supplementary documentation to inspect the reported schema; and (5) belong to the frozen comparator set selected independently of the eventual ARRP audit result.

Canonical selection table: `results/meta_analysis_selection_rule.csv`.

## Complete 35-cell ledger

The five ARRP descriptors were checked against each of seven syntheses, yielding 35 synthesis-by-dimension positions. The full evidence locations, reported coding/moderator fields, verdicts, and inference boundaries are in:

`results/meta_analysis_schema_field_audit.csv`

The audit found no dedicated reported A/D/O/G/N field in the frozen comparator set. This is a reported-schema readiness observation only. It does not show that primary studies omitted the information, that unpublished coder files lack it, that incompatible estimands were actually pooled, or that any published synthesis is biased or invalid.
